    How the numbers work

    A price of 35¢ on "Player X wins MVP" means traders put the chance at about 35%. Chance uses the midpoint between the best bid and ask when the spread is 10¢ or less, otherwise the last trade. Average is the mean of the markets that list the player. Fair odds are 1 ÷ chance, with no margin. Bookmaker odds include the bookmaker's margin, so they are usually shorter than fair odds.
